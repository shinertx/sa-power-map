## 1. Executive Summary & Regulatory Authority

The Department of Energy's **Title 17 Section 1706 Energy Infrastructure Reinvestment (EIR)** program provides up to **$250 Billion** in low-cost, long-term federal loan guarantees specifically designed to:
1. **Retool, repower, repurpose, or replace energy infrastructure that has ceased operations.**
2. **Enable operating energy infrastructure to run more efficiently and cleanly.**

Our target portfolio of **95 shuttered pulp, paper, and heavy manufacturing sites (holding 30.1 GW of live 138kV–345kV switchyards and on-site cogen)** meets the exact statutory definition of *"energy infrastructure that has ceased operations."*

## 2. Official DOE Site Control Rules (Do You Need the Site First?)

### ⚖️ The Official Rule:
* **Part I Application (Initial Eligibility & Intake):** You do **NOT** need to own the property outright. The DOE LPO requires **"demonstrable site control"**, which is officially satisfied by:
  1. A signed **Option Agreement to Purchase or Lease the Property / Project SPV**.
  2. A signed **Letter of Intent (LOI)** or Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with the property owner.
  3. A formal **Right of First Refusal (ROFR)** or exclusivity letter.
* **Part II Application & Conditional Commitment:** The full purchase of the site is executed **at financial close**, funded directly by the syndicate's equity and the DOE loan proceeds.

### Step 1: 90-Day Call Option with the Seller (Smurfit WestRock / Sonoco)
* Approach the corporate real estate disposition desk with an **Exclusive 90-Day Call Option Agreement**.
* Deposit **$50,000 to $100,000 into a third-party title escrow account**.
* Lock the fixed purchase price for the real estate and energized switchyard (e.g. $15M–$25M).
* **Benefit:** During these 90 days, nobody else can touch the site.

### Step 2: Offtake / Compute Partner LOI
* Secure a non-binding **Letter of Intent (LOI)** from an AI infrastructure partner (e.g., CoreWeave, Crusoe, an NVIDIA cloud partner, or private equity fund) confirming their intent to lease powered capacity upon energization.

### Step 3: DOE LPO Pre-Application Consultation
* Request a formal pre-application meeting with the DOE Loan Programs Office staff.
* **Cost:** $0 (No fee for pre-application consultation).
* Review project engineering, high-voltage interconnection capacity, and carbon emission reduction metrics with DOE project officers.

### Step 4: Part I & Part II Application Submission
* Submit project documentation through the DOE LPO online intake portal.
* Obtain a **Conditional Commitment** from the Secretary of Energy, unlocking federal financing at U.S. Treasury + 0.375% interest rates.

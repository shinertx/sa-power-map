3. Operating, Hardware & Capital Rules

  • Stay Under 39 MW Per Meter: Skips CPS Energy's 40 MW study and ERCOT's 75 MW review freeze.
  • State & Federal Sovereign Exemption: Under Tex. Loc. Gov't Code § 211.013(c), State university & federal land (UTSA, UT Health, TAMUSA, JBSA) is exempt from municipal zoning even inside San Antonio city limits.
  • Landlord Pay: Pay landlords a share per live megawatt ($0 rent until power turns on) and reimburse power at exact utility cost.
  • Pods & Financing: 480V pod quotes active with Vertiv, Dell, HPE, Eaton, and Torus; 5-year pod model live with Mike Whitaker's lender group and Dell Financial Services.

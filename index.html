How it works

Every site here is a guess until someone checks it. Your job is to turn guesses into proof.

  1. 1
    Find or add a siteSearch by address, building, broker or ZIP, click any dot on the map, or tap + Add Site to add a new address.
  2. 2
    Quick Update & MegawattsEnter the Megawatts (MW), tap a status pill, add your notes, and tap Submit Update.
  3. 3
    Share or verifyTap Share Link to text your update to the team, or open the optional transformer & breaker calculator for full electrical proof.

A site is validated when you mark it Validated or when the transformer is at least kVA, the main breaker is at least A at 480V 3-phase, spare power is at least MW, and fiber is confirmed. Your updates are saved on your phone and can be texted via Share Link.

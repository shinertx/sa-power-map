How it works

Every site here is a guess until someone checks it. Your job is to turn guesses into proof.

  1. 1
    Find a siteSearch by address, building, broker or ZIP. Or click any dot on the map.
  2. 2
    Do the desk checksLook at the satellite view, Street View and the listing sites. Answer yes or no. It takes a few minutes.
  3. 3
    Get the proofAsk the building for the transformer tag, the main breaker tag and a summer electric bill, and confirm fiber. Type in the numbers. The page does the maths.

A site is validated when the transformer is at least kVA, the main breaker is at least A at 480V 3-phase, spare power is at least MW, and fiber is confirmed. CPS Energy must still approve the added load. Your answers are saved in this browser. Use Export results to share them.

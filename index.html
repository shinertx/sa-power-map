How it works

Every site here is a guess until someone checks it. Your job is to turn guesses into proof.

  1. 1
    Find a siteSearch by address, building, broker or ZIP. Or click any dot on the map.
  2. 2
    Do the 4 desk checksLook at the satellite view and Street View. Answer yes or no. It takes about two minutes.
  3. 3
    Get the proofAsk the building for the transformer tag, the main breaker tag and a summer electric bill. Type in the numbers. The page does the maths.

A site is validated when the transformer is at least kVA, the main breaker is at least A at 480V 3-phase, and spare power is at least MW. Your answers are saved in this browser. Use Export results to share them.
